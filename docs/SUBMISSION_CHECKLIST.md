## Write-up ready to adapt

During extreme heat, community planners may have limited resources and multiple possible cooling locations. HeatReach provides a transparent way to explore which eligible proposals could offer the greatest additional vulnerability-weighted access under a budget and site cap.

The working React/TypeScript application uses exact subset enumeration, explicit overlap handling, deterministic tie-breaks and a matched first-eligible baseline. Users can alter budget, radius and availability, inspect the schematic map, and read an accessible zone table and per-site explanations. Domain, UI and real-browser tests reproduce the synthetic benchmark.

All demonstrated locations, populations, costs and weights are fictional. Results describe modelled straight-line reach, not operational facilities or achieved environmental or health impact.
